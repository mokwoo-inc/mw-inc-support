PhotoTidy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

PhotoTidy (the "app") is operated by Mokwoo Inc. (목우 주식회사, "we"). The app does not collect personal data. Photo analysis happens on your device; we run no server and receive neither your photos nor any information about them. The only things that leave your device are the Apple service requests listed under "What goes to Apple" below. There are no ads and no tracking.

What the app reads

  • Photo library: for the photos and videos you allow, the image, capture time, location stored in the photo (coordinates), file name, size, Favorite status and album membership. This is used to group similar photos and to find photos missing a location or time. If you allow selected photos only, only those are read.
  • The app does not read your device's current location and does not ask for location permission.

What the app changes

  • Deleting: only photos you mark to delete, and only after you allow it in iOS's confirmation. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. When deleting, a location, earlier capture time, Favorite status and album membership that only the deleted photos had are moved to the first photo you kept in that set.
  • Writing location and time: only suggestions you accept or values you enter yourself are written to that photo.

What happens on your device

Finding similar photos, judging sharpness and quality, and suggesting locations and times all happen on your device with Apple's built-in frameworks (such as Vision), and the results are not sent off the device.

What goes to Apple

We do not receive the content of these requests. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to them.

  • Place names (Apple geocoding): to show a place name on a location suggestion card or in the location editor, the coordinates of that place (from the suggested photo, or the spot you picked on the map) go to Apple.
  • Place search and maps (Apple Maps): searching for a place in the location editor sends your search text and the result you pick; viewing the map sends map requests for the visible area.
  • iCloud Photos: analyzing or viewing a photo whose original is not on the device sends a request to download it from your own iCloud.
  • App Store: purchases, restoring, purchase checks and code redemption are handled by the App Store. Codes are entered in the App Store sheet; neither the app nor we receive codes or payment information.
  • Rating requests: after photos are actually deleted (at most once every 14 days), the app may ask the system to show the rating prompt. iOS decides whether to show it, and Apple handles the response.

The app checks only whether an internet connection exists (so it does not send iCloud requests while offline) and does not store or send any connection details.

What is stored on your device

All of the following stays on this device and is not sent to us.

  • Photo index (photo identifiers, file names, capture times, coordinates, computed feature values and sharpness), kept in app storage so the library is not analyzed from scratch on every launch.
  • Set list, keep and delete decisions and their history, skipped pairs, and write records for accepted locations and times, kept in app storage to resume where you left off and confirm writes finished.
  • Number of photos deleted in the last 60 days (time and count only) and the widget summary (sets left, deleted this week, analysis progress), kept in app storage and in the space shared by the app and its widget (App Group) for the Home Screen widget.
  • Settings, last tab and the date of the last rating request, kept in app settings (UserDefaults).
  • Free sets counted today, the date, and SHA-256 hashes of the counted sets' photo identifiers, kept in the iOS Keychain to count 5 free sets a day. They stay on this device only and are not synced with iCloud Keychain.

Diagnostic logs stay on the device in the standard iOS log and the app does not send them. If you agreed to share with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide crash reports to the developer; that is an iOS feature. If device backup (including iCloud Backup) is on, app storage may be included in your backup; we cannot access it.

What the app does not collect

No sign-up or sign-in. No ads, no reading of the advertising identifier, no tracking. No third-party SDKs such as analytics, advertising or crash reporting tools. No syncing between devices.

Personal data we handle, retention, sharing and outsourcing

The app does not collect personal data, so we hold no personal data from the app, and there is no sharing with third parties and no outsourcing of processing. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them once the matter is resolved and they are no longer needed.

Deletion

Data on your device is destroyed when you delete the app, as iOS removes its app storage, app settings and widget space. The daily free-set record (a date, a count and hashes) is in the Keychain and may remain on the device after you delete the app; erasing the device removes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Purchases by family members can wait for a parent or guardian's approval through Apple's Ask to Buy.

Your rights

All app data is on your device, and you can remove it yourself by deleting the app. To access, correct or delete an email you sent us, contact us at the address below.
