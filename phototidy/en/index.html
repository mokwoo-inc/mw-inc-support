PhotoTidy Support

An iPhone app that groups similar photos into sets, shows one set at a time, and deletes only the photos you mark. For photos missing a location or capture time, it suggests the value from a similar photo.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or feature requests, contact the developer at mokwooinc@gmail.com.

When reporting a bug, please include the app version (Settings › Version), your device model and iOS version, and what you did on which screen. You do not need to send photos.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get deleted photos back? Yes. Photos that PhotoTidy deletes go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. Open that album in Photos, select the photos and recover them. How long photos stay in Recently Deleted, and what happens after that, is up to the Photos app. PhotoTidy has no trash of its own.

Before deleting, iOS shows its own confirmation once. If you do not allow it, nothing is deleted and your marks stay. Photos you did not mark to delete are never deleted. When deleting, anything only the deleted photos had (a location the kept photo lacks, an earlier capture time, Favorite, album membership) is moved to the first photo you kept in that set.

How many sets can I tidy for free each day? You can tidy 5 sets a day for free in the Duplicates tab. A set counts only when you mark at least one photo to keep or delete and then move on to the next set; sets you only look through are not counted. You get 5 new sets after midnight in your device's time zone, and Settings › Free Sets Today shows how many are left. After your 5 sets for the day, a "5 more sets tomorrow" card takes the next place, and you can still go back to the sets you tidied that day. The Add Location & Time tab has no limit. There is no free trial and no subscription.

I added the wrong location or time. PhotoTidy writes only suggestions you accept or values you enter yourself, and there is no in-app way to undo a value it wrote. Open the photo's info in the Photos app to change the date, time or location, or to remove the location. Suggestions you skip are never written. If suggestions are often wrong, turn off Settings › Location & Time Suggestions or change Suggestion Range.

It says some photos were "Not Downloaded from iCloud". With iCloud Photos and Optimize Storage turned on, some photos have their originals only in iCloud. To analyze those photos, PhotoTidy downloads a small preview from iCloud (also over cellular). If there is no internet connection or iCloud does not respond, the photo is left out of sets and counted under "Not Downloaded from iCloud". Check your connection, then tap Download Again.

I see no sets, or very few. A set needs at least 2 similar photos. Settings › Similarity changes how closely photos must match. If you allowed access to selected photos only, choose more in Settings › Change Selected Photos. If you changed many photos, try Settings › Rescan Photo Library.

Pro

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase (₩11,000 in Korea; other countries see their price in the App Store) that removes the daily limit. It is not a subscription, so you never pay again. Buy it in Settings › PhotoTidy Pro (Lifetime) or on the "5 more sets tomorrow" card.

If Pro does not show on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Settings › Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account (the card has the same button). Redeem a code you received in the App Store sheet that Settings › Redeem Code opens, or in the App Store app. Family Sharing is not supported. "Waiting for Approval" means the purchase is waiting for a parent or guardian to approve Ask to Buy. Purchases, restoring and code redemption are handled by the App Store; request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
