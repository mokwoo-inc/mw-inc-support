Translate in Place Support

A Safari extension that translates web pages in place with Apple on-device translation.

Getting started. Open the app, click "Open Safari Settings", turn the extension on and allow it on all websites. Then click "Translate This Page" on the toolbar button.

FAQ

  • Translation does not work: check that the extension is on in Safari Settings › Extensions and allowed on that site. A language pair you use for the first time needs a language pack that macOS downloads, so you need a connection. Safari's own pages and unsupported languages are not translated.
  • What is free: up to 10 pages a day, translated with "Translate This Page" in the toolbar popup, ⌥⇧T, the right-click menu or by translating a selection. Hovering to see the original and holding Option to hear it read aloud are free too. Pro has no daily limit and translates every page as soon as it opens. You pay once, it is yours for good, and it is not a subscription.
  • Shortcut to turn translation on or off: ⌥⇧T (Option+Shift+T). If another app uses the same keys, you can change it in Safari Settings › Extensions.
  • Restore a purchase: click "Restore purchases" in the app's Pro section.
  • A site I want left alone: turn it off for that site from the toolbar popup.
  • Privacy: page content never leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.

System requirements: macOS 26.4 or later, Safari.
