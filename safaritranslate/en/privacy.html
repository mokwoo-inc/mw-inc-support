Translate in Place Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Translation. The text of the page you translate is processed on your Mac by Apple's on-device translation. Page content and addresses are not sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device. The following stays inside Safari and the app on this Mac and never leaves it.

  • Source and target languages, reading settings, and the names of sites where translation is off
  • Whether Pro is purchased and which kind (free, lifetime; kept in the app so it can draw its screen right away), and when a rating request was last shown
  • How many pages were translated for free today: a list of random identifiers made for each page and the date (to apply the free limit of 10 pages a day, in Safari's extension storage). Page addresses are not stored, and the list is emptied when the day changes. The last time the limit was reached (in the app group storage) is used only to skip a rating request that day.
  • Language pack download state: the language pair the app asked macOS to download, whether it is downloading, downloaded or failed, and when (in the app group storage shared by the app and the extension). Used only to show progress in the popup.
  • Translation progress per tab: each tab's translation state, how many paragraphs were translated, whether the whole page or a selection was translated, and the address of another-language version of the page found in that tab (in Safari's extension storage). Removed when the tab is closed.
  • How many pages have been fully translated and when the last one finished (in the app group storage; page addresses are not stored). Used only to decide when to ask for a rating.
  • Only if you started the earlier 30-day free trial: when that trial ends, and the latest time this Mac has seen, used to count the trial period (in the app group storage shared by the app and the extension). The record that the trial was started is itself an App Store purchase record.

This information is stored only on your device.

What Apple handles. macOS downloads translation language packs from Apple. Pro purchases are handled by the App Store; the developer receives no payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to those parts.

Website access. The extension asks for access to all websites because it must read and replace the text of the pages you translate. The text it reads is used for nothing but that translation.

Children. We do not collect information from children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its effective date.

Operator and email

Translate in Place is operated by Mokwoo Inc. (목우 주식회사); the privacy officer is the representative of Mokwoo Inc. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them once the matter is resolved and they are no longer needed. If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.
