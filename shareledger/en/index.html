ShareLedger Support

A household ledger for iPhone and Mac that a family keeps together.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or feature requests, contact the developer at mokwooinc@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, the device (iPhone or Mac) and its system version, which screen you were on and what you did, and whether it was a shared ledger or your own. You do not need to send record contents such as amounts or memos.

FAQ

Where are my records stored? On your device and in iCloud (CloudKit). The developer runs no server. An iPhone and a Mac signed in to the same Apple Account use the same ledger.

How do I use it with my family? In the ledger settings, invite a family member by the email address of their Apple Account, and you share the same ledger through iCloud. The invited person must be signed in to iCloud.

Can I take my records out or move them? You can export to CSV in Settings. On iPhone you can also import CSV and export or import a full backup file that includes photos. Files are saved where you choose.

What happens when I delete a ledger? Deleting a ledger you created also deletes it from your iCloud; if you are offline, it is deleted once you are connected. You can leave a ledger shared with you to remove it from your device; you must be online to leave.

How do I attach photos? On iPhone, choose from your photo library or take one with the camera. On Mac, choose a file with "Choose File…".
