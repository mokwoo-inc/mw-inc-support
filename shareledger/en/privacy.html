ShareLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

ShareLedger does not send any information to a server of the developer. Your records are stored only on your device and in iCloud (for a ledger shared with you, in the iCloud of the person who created it), and the developer cannot see them. There are no ads and no user tracking.

Information Stored

  • Ledger records: date, amount, category, memo, who it was spent for, who recorded it, budget, satisfaction/waste marks, recurring expense rules, and ledger settings such as categories and budgets. Stored on your device and in the iCloud (CloudKit) of the person who created the ledger.
  • Photos attached to records: only photos you choose or take yourself, stored with the ledger in the same iCloud.
  • Your name and the "for whom" names: stored with the records, and visible to the people who use the same ledger.
  • App settings and the summary shown in widgets (this month's total, amounts by day and category, budget, upcoming recurring expenses): stored only in the storage the app and its widgets share on your iPhone (an App Group), and never sent off the device.

Family Sharing

When you invite family, you share the same ledger through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) sharing. A person who accepts the invitation can see the ledger's records and attached photos, and can add and edit records. The email address you type to invite someone is passed to Apple to find their Apple Account and is not sent to a server of the developer. The app does not keep invited email addresses separately; participants' names and email addresses are read from Apple's sharing information and shown in the participant list. The invitation link goes only to the app or person you choose.

Exporting and Importing Files

CSV and full backup files are saved where you choose after you tap export (the Files app, AirDrop, another app, and so on). Importing reads only the file you pick. The app does not send or keep those files.

iPhone and Mac

iPhone and Mac use the same ledger, stored in the same iCloud. On Mac you attach a photo with "Choose File…", and only the file you pick is read. The camera and widgets are used on iPhone only.

Purchases

Pro purchases (monthly or yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase) are processed by the App Store. The developer does not receive payment details such as card numbers; the app only checks with Apple whether you have Pro.

Permissions

The camera is used only to take a photo to attach to an expense record on iPhone. For photos, only the photos you pick in the picker are passed to the app; the app does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Notifications are used only to remind you of recurring expenses, and permission is requested the first time you save a recurring expense with a reminder. These are local notifications scheduled on your device. Separately, Apple's push service (CloudKit) may send silent notifications to tell your device about changes in iCloud, without any server of the developer. You can change permissions at any time in the Settings app.

Rating Requests

The app may ask for a rating through a system prompt. Apple handles your response.

What We Do Not Collect

ShareLedger does not collect personal data, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or tracking data. It uses no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If you agree in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Deletion

When you delete a record in the app, it is also deleted from iCloud. When you delete a ledger you created, the ledger and its photos are deleted from your iCloud; if you are offline, this happens once you are connected. You can leave a ledger shared with you to remove it from your device; you must be online, and this device's copy is deleted after leaving completes. Deleting the app does not delete your ledgers from iCloud.
