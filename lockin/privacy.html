## English

Lockin ("the App") is a focus timer. During a session it listens through the microphone so you can say a distracting thought out loud and let it go, and it can show you your own face in a mirror so you notice when you reach for the phone.

Both of those are intimate. This policy tells you exactly where that material goes. The short version: it stays on your phone, and we never receive it.

What the App records

  • Your voice. While a focus session runs and listening is on, the App captures audio from the microphone.
  • Your face. If you turn on the mirror, the App draws the camera feed on screen. If you also turn on timelapse, it writes a compressed .mp4 of that session.
  • What you said, as text. Audio is passed to Apple's on-device speech models and comes back as text. That text is the transcript you can read afterwards.
  • How long you focused. Start time, end time, length, and how many lines you spoke.

Where it goes

The audio itself is never written to a file. It flows through memory into the transcriber and is gone. There is no recording to recover, export, or hand over — not by us, and not by anyone who compels us.

Transcripts, focus history and timelapse video are written to the App's private container on your device (group.mokwooinc.secondmallow). They are not uploaded. We operate no server that stores them and we have no way to read them.

Speech recognition runs on your device using Apple's Speech framework and the language models installed on it. Your speech is not sent to us for processing.

AI, and what it is allowed to read

When a session ends, the App asks an AI model to pull to-dos out of what you said, so you do not have to reread the whole transcript. This means your full transcript is passed to a language model. You should know exactly which one.

  • The model is Apple's on-device foundation model, reached through the Foundation Models framework (SystemLanguageModel). It ships with iOS and runs on the processor in your hand.
  • We do not use Private Cloud Compute, which is a separate Apple service behind a separate entitlement.
  • We do not use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other model provider. There is no API key in this app because there is no API to call.
  • Your transcript is not used to train any model, ours or Apple's. We have no training pipeline and no way to feed one.
  • If Apple Intelligence is unavailable or switched off on your device, extraction is skipped and your transcript is untouched.

The speech-to-text step is the same story: Apple's on-device speech models, running locally.

The model can be wrong. Treat anything it pulls out as a suggestion to check, not a record of what you said. The transcript is the record.

What syncs to your own iCloud

If you are signed into iCloud, the App writes a small settings record to Apple's iCloud key-value store so your setup follows you between your own devices:

  • Blocked domains and the opaque tokens for allowed apps
  • Blocking state and any temporary timer
  • Appearance, language, daily focus minutes for the contribution graph
  • Your invite code, an invite code you redeemed, and your remaining referral balance

This is your iCloud, under your Apple Account. We cannot read it. Transcripts, timelapse video and session records are not in it.

What we actually receive

One thing, and only if you use the friend-invite feature. The App writes to a public CloudKit database we operate:

  • When you first open the invite screen: a six-character invite code, and the CloudKit user record ID that created it.
  • When you redeem someone's code: your CloudKit user record ID, the code you entered, and the time.

The CloudKit user record ID is generated by Apple, is different for every app, and cannot be traced back to your Apple ID, your email, your name, or your identity in any other app. It exists so two installs can recognise each other. Nothing else is sent — not a transcript, not a focus record, not a to-do, not an identifier of your device.

If you never open the invite screen, we receive nothing at all.

"Public database" — what that word means here

The invite records live in a CloudKit public database. That word is Apple's, and it is worth being precise about it: public means the records are not scoped to one person's private iCloud, so any install of this app can look up a code and find its owner. That is the whole point — an invite has to be findable by the person who received it.

What is in those records is a six-character code, an app-scoped identifier, and a timestamp. There is no name, no email, no device identifier, no transcript, no focus history, and nothing that describes you. If someone read every record in that database they would learn that some anonymous installs invited other anonymous installs.

Nothing else the App produces is ever written to any database we operate, public or private.

Nobody is listening

The App records you talking to yourself, and it is worth saying plainly what does not happen with that.

No person at this company can hear or read it. No process scans it for keywords, sentiment, risk, or anything else. Nothing you say triggers an alert, a report, a referral, or a message to anyone. The App does not detect distress and will not respond to it.

The App is not a crisis service and no one is on the other end. If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line — in Korea, 자살예방상담전화 109; in the US, 988; elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists services by country.

## 한국어

Lockin(이하 "이 앱")은 집중 타이머입니다. 집중이 도는 동안 마이크로 소리를 받아 딴생각을 소리 내어 말하고 흘려보낼 수 있게 하고, 거울로 자기 얼굴을 비춰 폰에 손을 뻗는 순간을 스스로 알아채게 합니다.

둘 다 사적인 것입니다. 이 방침은 그것이 정확히 어디로 가는지 밝힙니다. 요약하면 기기 안에 남고 회사는 받지 않습니다.

이 앱이 기록하는 것

  • 목소리. 집중이 돌고 듣기가 켜져 있는 동안 마이크로 소리를 받습니다.
  • 얼굴. 거울을 켜면 카메라 화면을 그립니다. 타임랩스까지 켜면 그 세션의 압축된 .mp4 를 만듭니다.
  • 말한 것의 글자. 소리는 애플의 기기 내 음성 모델을 거쳐 글자로 돌아옵니다. 나중에 읽는 전사가 그것입니다.
  • 얼마나 집중했는지. 시작 시각, 끝 시각, 길이, 말한 줄 수.
